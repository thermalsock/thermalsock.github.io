Instruments

Web OscilloscopeLive
Real-time signal analysis

A real dual-trace oscilloscope in your browser. Time domain, XY/Lissajous, FFT spectrum, spectrogram, chromagram, and a harmonic tuner. Runs on your mic or audio interface; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Open Oscilloscope → Web
Modulus StudioLive
LFO & envelope modelling

Dial in LFOs, envelopes, performance controls, and random sources exactly like your hardware synth, then watch them combine in real time against a destination - filter cutoff, pitch, amp, PWM, and more.

Open Modulus Studio → Web
Loom NewLive
Modal chord & drone generator

A harmonic idea engine for ambient, drone, and psybient music - modal scales, open/quartal/cluster voicings, and non-functional movement styles, with real audio playback so you can hear a progression before you patch it.

Open Loom → Web
Transient LabLive
Ear & hand training

Scales, chords, and genre-based lessons - drone, psybient, ambient house, hauntology - with real-time MIDI note judging and audio analysis. Plug in a MIDI keyboard and train against real content packs.

Open Transient Lab → Web
Signal PathLive
Hardware rig mapper

An interactive wiring diagram for a real hardware studio - every device, every audio/MIDI/CV/power cable, plus a settings deep-dive per device. Built for practicing and running a live set without a laptop in sight.

Open Signal Path → Web
Sound DesignLive
Synth patch reference

A patch-programming guide for the Sequential Take 5 - oscillators, mixer, filter, envelopes, ADSR, and suggested effects, walked through for dozens of patches from analog bass to atmospheric drones.

Open Sound Design → Web
