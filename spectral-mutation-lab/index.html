Spectral Mutation Lab

Thermalsock Labs

A real-time FFT-based spectral processor that runs entirely in your browser. Splits your input into 2048 frequency bins, mutates them — freeze, morph, shift, reverse, smear, scatter — and recombines them into a new sound. Plug in your studio's audio interface, then start the engine below. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere; all processing happens locally in an AudioWorklet.
