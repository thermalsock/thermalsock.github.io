Sequencing

KeyStep Pro Arp Machine

Pattern generator, panel and sequencing school

A melody and rhythm generator for the Arturia KeyStep Pro, built on a pixel-measured recreation of its front panel. Seventeen styles, twelve phrasing modes, true polyrhythm, and one-button injection straight into the hardware over MIDI.

  • Generate 16 steps, confirm, develop to 64
  • Lock the notes that work and reroll the rest
  • Send it to the device without typing a step
  • Sequencing School — learn it by ear, through your own synth

About the studio

Thermalsock Labs is one person in a home studio, building small tools for the gear already on the desk. Most of them started because something was hard to tune, hard to see, or hard to remember.

Everything here runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is recorded, and nothing is sent anywhere — the audio never leaves your machine. There are no accounts and no tracking.

The tools are free. If one of them saves you an afternoon, buy me a coffee.
