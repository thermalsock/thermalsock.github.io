Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Thermalsock Labs is a small set of browser-based tools for sound and music experimentation. This page explains what happens to your data when you use them — the short version is: almost nothing leaves your device.

What these tools do locally

Web Oscilloscope, Sound Design, Signal Path, Modulus Studio, Loom, Ambient Bloom, and Transient Lab all run entirely in your browser. Where they use data:

  • Microphone / audio input (Oscilloscope, Modulus Studio's optional audio overlay, Ambient Bloom's optional audio reactivity, Transient Lab's audio analysis) — processed live in your browser for display only. Nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.
  • MIDI input (Transient Lab) — read directly by your browser to judge notes you play. Not transmitted anywhere.
  • Local storage — some tools save small preferences (like a color theme, or a Signal Path rig layout you build) in your browser's local storage, on your device only. This never leaves your browser and isn't accessible to us.
None of the tools on this site currently create accounts, require sign-in, or store anything on a server. If that changes for a future feature (for example, a cloud-sync option), this policy will be updated first, and that feature will be opt-in.

Analytics

As of this writing, this site does not run any analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, this section will say so, name the service, and explain what it collects.

Third-party links and embeds

This site links out to a few third-party services, each with their own separate privacy practices we don't control:

  • Buy Me a Coffee — if you choose to support the project, that transaction happens entirely on their site under their own privacy policy.
  • Steam / the App Store — links to Rudiment's store listings go to those platforms directly.
  • Google Fonts — this site loads fonts from Google's servers, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies

These tools don't set their own tracking cookies. Third-party embeds above (payment or storefront pages you're sent to) may use their own — that's between you and them once you leave this site.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects what data is collected, the "Last updated" date at the top will change and, where practical, a note will be added to the hub page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Buy Me a Coffee page's messaging, until a dedicated contact address is set up here.
