What is this?

Every day, everyone who visits this page sees the same one prompt — one small, specific, doable thing. Not advice, not a feed, not a thing to optimize. You do it, you tap one button, and the page actively tells you to leave. There's no infinite scroll here on purpose.

Nothing about you is tracked. Your streak lives only in your own browser's local storage — it's never sent anywhere, there's no account, and nobody but you can see it. If you clear your browser data, your streak resets. That's the trade-off for there being no account system at all.

What you did each day stays private on purpose — the share card only ever shows your streak, never the prompt itself, so sharing it never spoils anyone else's day.

One Small Thing — no account, no tracking beyond your own device, no reason to stay once you're done.
