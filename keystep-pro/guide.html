Learn it by ear, not by reading about it

Every idea below is one switch. A sequence loops, you flip a single variable while it plays, and the difference you hear is the definition. Both sides always use the same seed, the same key and the same register — only the thing under discussion changes.

Play it through your own hardware. A Take 5 or a Prophet will teach you more about gate length in four seconds than the browser synth can in an afternoon.
