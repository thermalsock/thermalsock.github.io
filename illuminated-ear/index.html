The Illuminated Ear

Thermalsock Labs

An ear-training game in the same invented manuscript hand as Ambient Bloom. The page plays you a short line of glyphs — heard, not read — and you play it back on a MIDI keyboard, your instrument, or your voice. Match it, and the page illuminates; miss a note, and a scribe's correction mark shows you where. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere; all listening happens locally in your browser.

Headphones recommended — like the rest of the site, echo cancellation is left off so it doesn't distort a real instrument's signal, which also means your speakers can bleed back into an open mic during playback.

Perceptual Upgrades

120 XP to Level 2

Correct notes earn XP toward new hint types shown just before each note plays — a wrong note still keeps whatever XP you'd already earned that round. Levels 4-6 (triad, melody, and rhythm vision) aren't built yet.

How it's scored

Each round plays a short sequence of notes, then goes quiet — play it back on your instrument, voice, or MIDI keyboard. A correct sequence lights up the page and the next round is one note longer. A wrong note ends the round early and shows you which one broke the chain; the next round backs off by one note rather than resetting to the start.
