An open notebook that writes itself as you play. Every note leaves a wedge-stroke mark; loud hits leave ink blots; a fast passage fills the page quickly, a slow pad fills it gradually. When a page is full, it turns, and the book keeps writing. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — it only ever exists on the page while you're listening.
